## HamSCI-Supplied PSWS Receivers
HamSCI has some support from NASA and the National Science Foundation to supply PSWS hardware to schools and institutions. If you have received this hardware, most of the steps in this documentation are taken care of, but you should still read over the [Site Requirements](https://hamsci.github.io/HFRx/preparation/#site-requirements) and [Antenna](https://hamsci.github.io/HFRx/antenna/) sections. Check out the [Prebuilt Station Assembly](https://hamsci.github.io/HFRx/prebuilt/) page for instructions on setting up your station.
