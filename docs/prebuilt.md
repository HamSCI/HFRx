## Prebuilt Stations
If you've received a prebuilt station funded by NSF or NASA, follow the assembly instructions on this page. 

### DXE-AVA-3 Connections
 - 75-ohm output → RG-6 cable → SMA Male to F-Type Female coaxial cable assembly (RG316, 50 Ω) → Shelf +30/60 MHz Low Pass Filter.
 - Optional DC power output → RG-6 cable → F-Type Female Jack to RCA Female Adapter → 5.5 mm × 2.1 mm DC Power to RCA Adapter Cable → Jameco 12 V power supply

### Additional notes:
 - The GPS antenna is already connected; just place it near a window.
 - Connect the USB hub to the Beelink's USB-C port.
 - Connect the RX888 and Pi Eliminator to the USB hub.
 - The fan is optional and can be connected to a front USB port.
 - Connect the keyboard to the second USB port.
 - After securing the plexiglass, place the Beelink on top and connect the GPSDO and TS1 USB cables to the rear USB ports.
 - Connect the CAT6 cable between the PVC housing and the Pi Eliminator.
 - Use the included HDMI cable to connect the monitor.
